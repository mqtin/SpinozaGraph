A given action is called bad, in so far as it arises from one being affected by
hatred or any evil emotion. But no action, considered in itself alone, is either
good or bad (as we pointed out in the preface to Pt. IV.), one and the same
action being sometimes good, sometimes bad; wherefore to the action which is
sometimes bad, or arises from some evil emotion, we may be led by reason (IV.
xix.). Q.E.D.
