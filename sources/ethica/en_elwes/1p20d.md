God (by the last Prop.) and all his attributes are eternal, that is (by Def.
viii.) each of his attributes expresses existence. Therefore the same attributes
of God which explain his eternal essence, explain at the same time his eternal
existence&#x2013;in other words, that which constitutes God's essence constitutes at
the same time his existence. Wherefore God's existence and God's essence are one
and the same. Q.E.D.
