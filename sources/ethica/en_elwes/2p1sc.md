This proposition is also evident from the fact, that we are able to conceive an
infinite thinking being. For, in proportion as a thinking being is conceived as
thinking more thoughts, so is it conceived as containing more reality or
perfection. Therefore a being, which can think an infinite number of things in
an infinite number of ways, is, necessarily, in respect of thinking, infinite.
As, therefore, from the consideration of thought alone, we conceive an infinite
being, thought is necessarily (Pt. i., Deff. iv. and vi.) one of the infinite
attributes of God, as we were desirous of showing.
