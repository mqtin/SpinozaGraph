In proportion as the parts of an individual, or a compound body, are in contact
over a greater or less superficies, they will with greater or less difficulty
admit of being moved from their position; consequently the individual will, with
greater or less difficulty, be brought to assume another form. Those bodies,
whose parts are in contact over large superficies, are called hard; those, whose
parts are in contact over small superficies, are called soft; those, whose parts
are in motion among one another, are called fluid.
