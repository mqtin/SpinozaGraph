The essence of the mind only affirms that which the mind is, or can do; in other
words, it is the mind's nature to conceive only such things as assert its power
of activity (last Prop.). Thus, when we say that the mind contemplates its own
weakness, we are merely saying that while the mind is attempting to conceive
something which asserts its power of activity, it is checked in its
endeavour-&#x2014;in other words (III. xi. note), it feels pain. Q.E.D.
