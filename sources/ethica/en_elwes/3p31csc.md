This endeavour to bring it about, that our own likes and dislikes should meet
with universal approval, is really ambition (see III. xxix. note); wherefore we
see that everyone by nature desires (appetere), that the rest of mankind should
live according to his own individual disposition: when such a desire is equally
present in all, everyone stands in everyone else's way, and in wishing to be
loved or praised by all, all become mutually hateful.
