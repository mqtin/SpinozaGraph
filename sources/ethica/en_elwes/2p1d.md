Particular thoughts, or this and that thought, are modes which, in a certain
conditioned manner, express the nature of God (Pt. i., Prop. xxv., Coroll.). God
therefore possesses the attribute (Pt. i., Def. v.) of which the concept is
involved in all particular thoughts, which latter are conceived thereby.
Thought, therefore, is one of the infinite attributes of God, which express
God's eternal and infinite essence (Pt. i., Def. vi.). In other words, God is a
thinking thing. Q.E.D.
